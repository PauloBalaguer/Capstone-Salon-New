    After you request

    Rescheduling & Reminders

    How to reschedule
    1. Log in and open My Account → Home Service.
    2. Open “Need a different date? Reschedule online” on your request.
    3. Pick a new date and time at least 3 days away, then tap Move my booking.
    4. You'll get an email confirmation of the new schedule. Your assigned team is notified too.

    No account? Call 0918 536 8016 to move your request. To check its status, tap Track your booking and enter your reference (e.g. LM-HOM-2026-000041).

    Important reminders & rules
    • •Reschedule online at least 3 days (72 hours) before your event. Closer than that, please call 0918 536 8016.
    • •You can move a request online up to 2 times. After that, contact the branch.
    • •If stylists are already assigned, the whole team must be free on the new date, otherwise pick another date.
    • •Your quote and reservation fee carry over to the new date. Reservation fees are non-refundable reschedule instead of cancelling.
    • •Your request is confirmed only after the reservation fee is paid (pay online via GCash, Maya or card from the email link or Track your booking).
    • •Requests already in progress, completed or cancelled can't be rescheduled online.
    • •All updates, quote, payment link and schedule changes, are sent to your email. Check your spam folder if you don't see them.
