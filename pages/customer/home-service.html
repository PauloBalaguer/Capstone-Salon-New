How payment works

  1. No payment now — just send this request.
  2. We review it and send your quote with a reservation fee (DP) by notification and email.
  3. Pay the DP online via GCash, Maya or card (PayMongo) from this page — your request is then confirmed automatically.
  4. Pay the remaining balance on the event day.

The final amount is confirmed in your quote.

The DP is non-refundable, but it carries over if you reschedule (online, at least 3 days before the event).
